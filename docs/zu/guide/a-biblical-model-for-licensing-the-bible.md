# Isibonelo SeBhayibheli Sokunikeza Ilayisensi IBhayibheli

**“...namukele ngesihle; yiphani ngesihle.” - Mathewu 10:8**

AmaKristu akholelwa ukuthi amazwi kaJesu angamazwi kaNkulunkulu uqobo futhi anikezwa isintu mahhala ngezindleko ezinkulu kuJesu ngokwakhe owashiya yonke induduzo ukuze athwale inyama yomuntu futhi ahlanganyele ekuhluphekeni kwethu.

Ngokufanayo, makube kude nathi ukudala noma yiziphi izindonga zokukhokha noma ezinye izithiyo ezivimbela abanye ekudaleni okuhlangenwe nakho okukhazimulisa izwi likaNkulunkulu.

Ekufuneni kwethu ukuthola umlingani owayezimisele ukwabelana ngenguqulo yeBhayibheli yesiNgisi ephelele nesezingeni eliphezulu nomhlaba wonke mahhala futhi ngaphandle kwemingcele yokusetshenziswa nge-API, ngeshwa sithole abalingani abambalwa kuphela abazimisele ukwenza kanjalo. Okuphawulekayo ukuthi sibonga kakhulu [ithimba leBhayibheli laseBerean](https://berean.bible/) elenze le API yaba nokwenzeka.

Ukudalwa kweBhayibheli laseBereya kwazalwa ngenxa yesidingo njengoba ubuholi babuhlinzeke [ngeBibleHub](https://biblehub.com/) njengenkonzo emphakathini jikelele futhi baba neminyaka eminingi yesipiliyoni sababhali bamalayisense abanikeza amalayisense bese behoxisa amalayisense uma izibuyekezo zibuyekezwa, imikhawulo yesikhathi isiphela, kanye nezinye izinkinga eziningi ezihlobene. Ekugcineni ithimba leBibleHub lanquma ukudala inguqulo yalo ukuqinisekisa ukuthi inguqulo yesiNgisi ingasetshenziswa ngokukhululekile umhlaba wonke ngaphandle kwemingcele yomshicileli noma yababhalisile.

Uma unezinye izinguqulo zeBhayibheli noma wazi ezinye ezizimisele ukwenza imibhalo itholakale mahhala futhi ngaphandle kwemingcele yokusetshenziswa sicela usithinte [ku-hello@helloao.org](mailto:hello@helloao.org)

Okokugcina, sithandazela wonke umuntu onelungelo lokushicilela noma umshicileli wemibhalo yeBhayibheli ovimbela ukusetshenziswa kwayo ngelayisensi ngokungathi sína futhi acabangele ngokucophelela lokho uJesu angase akutshele bona lapho ebuya.

Ngiyabonga!

-Ithimba le-AO Lab

## Ilayisensi Yakho Yale Mibhalo

Imibhalo yeBhayibheli laseBereya kanye neBhayibheli eliningi [inikezelwe ngokusemthethweni emphakathini](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) kusukela ngomhlaka-30 Ephreli 2023.

_Zonke izindlela zokusebenzisa zivunyelwe mahhala._

### Isaziso Sokunikezwa (siyaziswa kodwa asidingeki):

IBhayibheli Elingcwele, iBerean Standard Bible, i-BSB ikhiqizwe ngokubambisana neBible [Hub](https://biblehub.com/) , [iDiscovery Bible](https://discoverybible.com/) , [i-OpenBible.com](https://openbible.com/) , kanye neBerean Bible Translation Committee. Lo mbhalo weZwi likaNkulunkulu [unikezelwe emphakathini](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) .
