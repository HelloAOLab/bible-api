# Misalin Littafi Mai Tsarki don Lasisi a Littafi Mai Tsarki

**"... kun karɓa kyauta; ku bayar kyauta." - Matta 10:8**

Kiristoci sun yi imani cewa kalmomin Yesu kalmomin Allah ne kuma an ba su kyauta ga ɗan adam a kan babban farashi ga Yesu da kansa wanda ya bar kowace ta'aziyya don ɗaukar jikin ɗan adam da kuma raba cikin wahalarmu.

Haka nan, ya yi nesa da mu mu ƙirƙiri duk wani shingen biyan kuɗi ko wasu shinge da ke hana wasu ƙirƙirar abubuwan da ke ɗaukaka kalmar Allah.

A cikin bincikenmu na neman abokin tarayya wanda ke son raba cikakken fassarar Littafi Mai Tsarki ta Turanci ga duniya kyauta kuma ba tare da ƙuntatawa ta amfani da API ba, abin baƙin ciki, mun sami wasu abokan tarayya kaɗan ne kawai da ke son yin hakan. Abin lura na musamman, muna matuƙar godiya fiye da kalmomi ga [ƙungiyar Berean Bible](https://berean.bible/) da ta sa wannan API ya yiwu.

Ƙirƙirar Littafi Mai Tsarki na Berean ya samo asali ne daga buƙatar da shugabannin suka yi, domin sun samar da [Littafi Mai Tsarki (BibleHub)](https://biblehub.com/) a matsayin hidima ga jama'a, kuma suna da gogewa ta shekaru da dama a fannin masu ba da lasisi, suna ba da lasisin soke lasisi bayan an sabunta su, ƙayyadadden wa'adi, da sauran batutuwa masu alaƙa. A ƙarshe, ƙungiyar BibleHub ta yanke shawarar ƙirƙirar fassarar tasu don tabbatar da cewa duniya za ta iya amfani da fassarar Turanci ba tare da takunkumin mawallafi ko mai ba da lasisi ba.

Idan kana da ko ka san wasu fassarorin Littafi Mai Tsarki waɗanda suma suke son samar da rubuce-rubuce kyauta kuma ba tare da ƙuntatawa ba, tuntuɓi mu a [hello@helloao.org](mailto:hello@helloao.org)

A ƙarshe, muna addu'ar duk wanda ke da haƙƙin mallaka ko kuma mai buga littattafan Littafi Mai Tsarki da ya takaita amfani da su ta hanyar lasisi da gaske kuma ya yi la'akari da abin da Yesu zai iya faɗa musu idan ya dawo.

Na gode!

-   Ƙungiyar Lab ta AO

_Ana ba da izinin amfani da duk wani abu da aka yarda da shi kyauta._

## game da Mu

[AO Lab](https://helloao.org/) kamfani ne mai zaman kansa wanda ya sadaukar da kansa don ƙauna da kuma rayuwa da Kalmar Allah. Manufar wannan aikin ita ce a samar da Littafi Mai Tsarki kyauta ga duk wanda ya kamata ya buƙaci shi a cikin tsarin da aka inganta don amfani da shi ta hanyar aikace-aikace.

Muna farin cikin yin haɗin gwiwa da [ƙungiyar BSB](https://bereanbibles.com/) . Ba tare da taimakonsu da goyon bayansu ba, wannan API ɗin ba zai kasance ba.

Idan kuna son ƙarin bayani game da BSB, [danna nan](https://bereanbibles.com/about-berean-study-bible/) .

Ziyarci [HelloAO.org](https://helloao.org/) don ƙarin koyo game da sauran ayyukanmu da kuma yadda za ku iya shiga.
