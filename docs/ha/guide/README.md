# Gabatarwa

Littafi Mai Tsarki, wanda shine kalmar Allah da aka hure, shine mafi mahimmancin tarin takardu a tarihin duniya. An yi nazarinsa tsawon tsararraki marasa adadi ta hanyar al'adu daga ko'ina cikin duniya, kuma yana ɗauke da gaskiya da yawa waɗanda aka gina dukkan rayuwarmu a kai.

## game da Mu

[AO Lab](https://helloao.org/) kamfani ne mai zaman kansa wanda ya sadaukar da kansa don ƙauna da kuma rayuwa da Kalmar Allah. Manufar wannan aikin ita ce samar da Littafi Mai Tsarki (da albarkatun da suka shafi hakan) kyauta ga duk wanda ya kamata ya buƙaci shi a cikin tsarin da aka inganta don amfani da shi ta hanyar aikace-aikace.

Muna farin cikin yin haɗin gwiwa da [ƙungiyar BSB](https://bereanbibles.com/) . Ba tare da taimakonsu da goyon bayansu ba, wannan API ɗin ba zai kasance ba.

Idan kuna son ƙarin bayani game da BSB, [danna nan](https://bereanbibles.com/about-berean-study-bible/) .

## Bayani Game da Fassara

Da farko an rubuta Littafi Mai Tsarki da Ibrananci, Girkanci da Aramaic, yanzu ana amfani da shi azaman tarin fassara zuwa harsunan zamani masu shahara kamar Turanci, Jamusanci, da Sifaniyanci. Saboda tsoffin Ibrananci da Girkanci sun bambanta da harsunan mu na zamani, akwai shawarwari da yawa da mai fassara dole ne ya yanke domin ya fassara Littafi Mai Tsarki daidai da daidai a cikin harshen zamani kamar Turanci. Sakamakon haka, fassarar a ƙarshe tsari ne na fassara mai ƙirƙira wanda ke buƙatar zurfafa bincike da mahallin tarihi. Saboda haka, babu fassarar Littafi Mai Tsarki ta ƙarshe zuwa harshen zamani. Wannan ba ta rage ingancin nassosi gabaɗaya ba, amma yana barin mu a buɗe ga haɗarin fahimtar manufar marubucin asali idan muka karanta fassara ɗaya kawai.

A yau an yi fassarori daban-daban na Littafi Mai Tsarki, kowannensu yana ƙoƙarin hanyoyi daban-daban da kuma ba da nauyi ga ra'ayoyi daban-daban domin a yi ƙoƙarin fahimtar Littafi Mai Tsarki ta hanyar da ta dace da kuma daidai. Kowane mai fassara yana neman ya sa Littafi Mai Tsarki ya zama mai sauƙin fahimta a cikin wani harshe, amma suna iya ɗaukar hanyoyi daban-daban don cimma hakan.

::: tip
Misali, masu fassara a tarihi dole ne su yanke shawara tsakanin ɗaukar hanyar fassara ta "kalmomi-kalma" inda suke ƙoƙarin gano ma'anonin asali na kalmomin Ibrananci da Girkanci da kuma sanya daidai Ingilishi/Sifaniyanci/da sauransu, ko kuma hanyar "tunani-don-tunani" inda suke ƙoƙarin nemo ainihin ma'anar jimloli da ra'ayoyi sannan su fassara ma'ana iri ɗaya.

Yawancin fassarorin zamani suna ɗaukar hanyoyi daban-daban don ƙoƙarin cimma wani abu da yake jin kamar na halitta a cikin harshen da aka nufa, yayin da kuma suke kiyaye daidaiton asalin.
:::

Yana da muhimmanci a lura cewa yawancin fassarorin sun yi daidai da abubuwan da ke ciki, amma zurfafa karatu kan kowace fassara ta musamman na iya zama abin ɓatarwa domin akwai ma'anoni da ƙa'idodi da ke akwai a cikin harshen asali waɗanda ƙila za su yi wuya a wakilta a cikin fassarar, haka kuma an ba da shawarar ra'ayoyi da ke wanzuwa a cikin harshen fassara waɗanda ƙila ba su zama ainihin manufar marubucin asali ba idan ba a yi nazari a hankali ba.

Lokacin karanta Littafi Mai Tsarki, yana da muhimmanci ka tambayi kanka abin da kake ƙoƙarin fahimta, sannan ka zaɓi fassara ɗaya ko fiye da za ta iya taimaka maka ka cimma aikinka. Misali, idan kawai kana karanta Littafi Mai Tsarki ne don jin daɗi da fahimta ta asali, to ka zaɓi kowace fassara ta zamani kamar fassarar [BSB](https://bereanbibles.com/) , [ESV](https://www.esv.org/) , ko [NASB](https://www.lockman.org/new-american-standard-bible-nasb/) . Duk da haka, idan kana ƙoƙarin fahimtar wani takamaiman jumla ko ma'ana, to ya fi kyau ka kwatanta fassarori da yawa da juna ko ma ka ɗauki littafi mai layi wanda zai sauƙaƙa maka ganin tushen Ibrananci/Girkanci na wani sashe.

Amma mafi muhimmanci duka shine addu'a don hikimar jagoranci ta Ruhu Mai Tsarki, domin a cikin kalmomin Yesu: **"... Shi, Ruhu na gaskiya, zai shiryar da ku cikin dukkan gaskiya." - Yohanna 16:13**
