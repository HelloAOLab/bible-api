# API

Takardun da aka yi amfani da su don Amfani da Littafi Mai Tsarki Kyauta, an tsara su ta rukuni:

-   [**Fassara, Littattafai, & Babi**](./translations/README.md) - Fassarorin Littafi Mai Tsarki, littattafansu, da abubuwan da ke cikin babi, waɗanda ake samu a cikin tsari [na yau da kullun](./translations/standard.md) da kuma [sauƙi](./translations/simplified.md) .
-   [**Sharhin**](./commentaries/README.md) Littafi Mai Tsarki - Sharhin Littafi Mai Tsarki, littattafansu, surori, da bayanan martaba.
-   [**Saitin Bayanai**](./datasets/README.md) - ƙarin bayanai, kamar nassoshi tsakanin harsuna da kuma abubuwan da ke cikin Littafi Mai Tsarki (mutane, wurare, abubuwan da suka faru, da ƙungiyoyin mutane), da littattafansu, surori, da kuma abubuwan da ke cikinsu.
-   [**OpenAPI**](./openapi.md) - Takardar OpenAPI wadda ke bayanin API, wadda za ku iya amfani da ita don samar da abokan ciniki don wasu harsuna.
