# Wordproject licenses

This is the copyright research behind the license fields of the Wordproject catalog in [`wordproject.ts`](./wordproject.ts) (`licenseStatus`, `identifiedAs`, `publicDomain` and `includeByDefault`). It covers the 57 texts that the API doesn't already have. The 30 texts that the API already has (see `duplicateOf`) weren't checked.

Wordproject doesn't state a license for its texts. Its [copyright statement](https://www.wordproject.org/contact/new/copyrights.htm) says it publishes them in good faith as texts that are believed to be in the public domain, claiming fair use. Most of them aren't: many are modern Bible Society editions, and several Indian language texts are the Easy-to-Read Version, which is explicitly not in the public domain.

## How the texts were checked

Each text was identified from its own wording (Genesis 1:1, John 3:16 and other verses), not from Wordproject's labels, which are sometimes wrong. The strongest evidence is word for word matches with texts whose licenses are known: the [CrossWire SWORD modules](https://crosswire.org/sword/modules/) and their `.conf` files, a copy of the Easy-to-Read Version texts with their license file, and the Free Use Bible API itself. ebible.org, bible.com, Wikipedia and find.bible couldn't be opened during the research (November 2026), so many facts come from search result summaries of those sites. Those are linked below but weren't read in full, and the `low` and `medium` confidence entries should be checked again against them.

Publication dates are judged against the United States rule that works published before 1931 are in the public domain (as of 2026), and against the term of the country the text comes from where that is known.

## Included by default

These 13 public domain texts, and the Amharic Bible (`amh_amh`), are downloaded by `fetch-wordproject-translations` when no translations are given.
